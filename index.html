Openclaw-gog

Openclaw-gog is a private integration between OpenClaw and Google services. It is used by the owner of the OpenClaw environment and explicitly authorized Google accounts to perform tasks involving email, calendars, contacts, and business documents stored in Google Drive.

Functionality

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, the integration may access:

  • Google Drive
  • Google Docs
  • Google Sheets
  • Gmail
  • Google Calendar
  • Google Contacts

Access to Google data is used only to carry out instructions from an authorized user, such as locating a document, reading a file, checking calendar information, or working with email messages.

Private Use

Openclaw-gog is not a public SaaS product and is not offered as a service to third parties. It is a private automation integration used by the owner of the OpenClaw environment.
